# ABC Lima Dasar

An online version of the traditional Indonesian children's word game ("ABC lima dasar"). Players
show 1–10 fingers, the sum becomes a letter, everyone races to type a word in the chosen category
that starts with that letter, and the other players vote out bad answers. Guests only: no accounts,
just a nickname per browser tab.

## Game rules (current)

- 2–8 players; the host can't start with fewer than 2 online. A running game ends when fewer than 2 remain (after a 30 s reconnect grace).
- Waiting room: every online player other than the host presses "Siap" before the host can start (the host counts as ready; readiness resets after each game). The host can ping a player who isn't ready (sound `client/public/sounds/ping.mp3`, from Pixabay under the Pixabay Content License: free to use, no attribution; + vibration on Android, once per 10 s per player) and kick players, in the waiting room only (`onMemberLeave` rejects a kick mid-game); a kicked player may join again.
- Up to 10 rounds; the host picks the category (Negara, Hewan, Kota, Buah & Sayur, Profesi, Makanan & Minuman, or a custom "Lainnya"), round count, and answering time (slider: 20, 25, 30, 35 or 40 s; default 30; `answerSeconds`, validated by the server).
- Picking takes 10 s. Letter = `(sum − 1) % 26`; a letter already used skips to the next unused one.
- The reveal lasts up to 10 s (20 s let players look answers up in another tab): each player picks "Ganti huruf" or "Tetap". More than half wanting a change swaps the letter for a random unused one (once per round); half or more keeping it starts answering at once.
- Answering takes the room's answering time (default 30 s):
  - One answer each, in Indonesian (KBBI loanwords count, even when spelled like English: editor, senator; unabsorbed English like "chef" does not), letters and spaces only, no duplicates within a game. Duplicates are compared ignoring spaces, and other names of the same thing count as the same answer (`sameAnswerKey`, `same` maps in `negara.json` and `makanan-minuman.json`; an alias shared by two countries, like "kongo", isn't mapped).
  - Paste is blocked, and answers within 1 s of the phase start are rejected.
  - Answers with swear words are rejected (`INAPPROPRIATE_ANSWER`, counts toward the retry pause) using the base `indonesian-badwords` dictionary + English, so "Anjing"/"Babi" still work; verified answers are never checked. Chat and nicknames use the strict expanded dictionary (`createProfanityChecker({ strict })` in `backend/src/modules/chat/profanity.ts`); nicknames are also checked with digits/`_`/`-` turned into spaces (`INAPPROPRIATE_NAME`). The dictionaries only match whole words, so every checker also strips common suffixes (-nya, -mu, -ku, -lah, -kah, -pun) and flags words that start with a base-dictionary swear word of 5+ letters ("kontolx") or one of a short hand-picked list of English roots (`ENGLISH_PREFIXES`: "fuckx", "shitty"; the full English list as prefixes would hit Indonesian words like analisis); a test keeps every category word list (except Kota, all verified) free of hits.
  - The last player left gets a short final countdown.
- Every countdown bar turns red-pen for its last 3 s.
- Voting takes 20 s:
  - Answers found in the category's word list are **verified**: they pass without a vote and can't be rejected; voting is skipped when every answer is verified. Lists live in `backend/src/modules/abc-words/words/` and are built by `backend/scripts/word-lists/*.ts` (Negara from CLDR, Kota from GeoNames CC BY 4.0, Hewan from Wordnet Bahasa (MIT) + PWN noun.animal, filtered, plus a hand-written list, Buah & Sayur hand-written, Profesi hand-written and single-word only by the owner's rule ("Dokter" yes, "Dokter Gigi" goes to the vote; juru/kepala/ahli/pemandu/penjaga/pegawai/anggota + X are left out, single-word synonyms like koki, nutrisionis, pramuwisata are in; only pemadam kebakaran, ibu rumah tangga and wali kota are two words; Makanan & Minuman hand-written, single words or reduplications only plus seven approved two-word names (es krim, es teh, es campur, air putih, ayam goreng, pisang goreng, bika ambon), no fruit/vegetables (own category) or alcohol, with a `same` map for spellings of one dish like mi/mie); sources credited on the rules page). Answers not in a list still go to the vote, never auto-fail. In Hewan and Buah & Sayur only base names are verified: "jambu biji" or "ikan emas" go to the vote because "jambu"/"ikan" are listed (`dropQualified`; reduplications like "kura kura" and different animals like "kuda nil" are kept). Negara and Kota keep their multi-word names (Korea Selatan, Padang Panjang are different places).
  - Rejecting needs a reason (off-category / misspelled / not-a-word), and votes are public.
  - An answer fails only with **at least 2 rejections and more than half of the other online players**. With 3+ online, one player can never fail an answer alone.
  - Two online (a 2-player game, or only two still connected): the other player is the only judge (`judgedByOne`). One rejection fails an unverified answer, but for 0 points (`POINTS.rejectedByOne`) instead of −1, the rejection is not refunded (so at most 3 answers per game can be blocked), and the lone-rejection rule doesn't apply. Verified answers still pass, and "Lainnya" stays playable.
  - Each player has 3 rejections per game, refunded when the answer fails with support.
- Ranking: score, then the faster average answer time (accepted answers only, ms from the start of answering, measured by the server). Equal on both shares the rank.
- Points: accepted +1, failed −1 (was −2; a failed answer is often just a typo; 0 when only one player judged it), no answer −1. Rejecting alone an answer that still passes is free the 1st time, a ⚠ 2/3 warning the 2nd, and −1 from the 3rd on. Scores never go below 0.
- Anti-abuse:
  - Answer spam: 3 rejected tries in 5 s pauses answering for 3 s.
  - Chat spam: mute.
  - AFK: no pick and no answer for 2 rounds, after which the game stops waiting for that player.

## Design system: "buku tulis" (notebook paper)

- Phone first (check 375 px and 768 px). One light theme, also on dark-mode devices. Content column max-w-3xl on a fluid paper background.
- Tokens are in `client/src/app/globals.css`:
  - paper `#fdfbf4`, paper-line `#cfddf0`, margin `#eba0a0`
  - ink `#1d2b5c`, plus ink-soft and ink-faint
  - redpen `#d1362a`, highlight `#ffe34d`, highlight-soft `#fff4b0`, success
  - Classes `.paper` and `.note-card`.
- Type: Fredoka (bold, dark) everywhere; Kalam handwriting only for "teacher's red-pen" notes.
- Conventions:
  - Red pen marks the round's letter: stamped and circled with `components/pen-circle.tsx` when it is revealed; afterwards (answering, voting, result) it is a plain red letter, since a circle around I or J read as a symbol.
  - Used letters and failed answers are crossed out diagonally in red.
  - The highlighter marks what is selected, your own items and the winner.
  - Child-hand images 1–10 (`client/public/hands/`) replace tally marks.
  - Gold, silver and bronze trophy stickers (`client/src/assets/trophies/`) mark ranks 1–3.
  - Empty seats are one dashed cell, "+N bangku kosong" (listing every empty seat pushed the waiting room off a phone screen).
  - Waiting room on phones: non-hosts see the rules as one summary line; chat is a floating speech-bubble button with an unread count that opens a bottom sheet (`chat-dock.tsx`, every phase); wider screens keep the inline chat card.
- Motion: one orchestrated moment per screen, and everything is off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Keep one bold element per screen. Keep images light.
