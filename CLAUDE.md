# ABC Lima Dasar

An online version of the traditional Indonesian children's word game ("ABC lima dasar"). Players
show 1–10 fingers, the sum becomes a letter, everyone races to type a word in the chosen category
that starts with that letter, and the other players vote out bad answers. Guests only: no accounts,
just a nickname per browser tab.

## Working with the owner

- Reply in **Indonesian**. Code, comments and commit messages stay in English (backend comments are Indonesian; match the file).
- **Show before integrating**: for visual work or new assets, show a screenshot/preview first and wait for an OK.
- **Commit and push only when asked.** Work on `develop` in this repo; merge to `main` only after the owner says so. The backend is a separate repo (see below) on `feature/abc-words`; "push" means push both.
- Images are generated by the owner from prompts Claude writes (use the hex colours below, transparent background). Trim and compress what comes back (webp, ~10 KB per sticker). Keep intentional details such as the trophies' different sizes.
- For UX questions give two options and a recommendation; let the owner choose.
- Personal project, but always apply best practice on both sides: server-side validation, typed contracts, backend tests, typecheck + lint before committing, check in the browser at phone width.

## Game rules (current)

- 2–8 players; the host can't start with fewer than 2 online. A leave that drops the room below 2 ends a running game at once (see "Stopped early").
- Fewer than 2 online mid-game (owner, 2026-11-26/27): the game waits up to 30 s (`abandonMs` = `WAIT_FOR_PLAYERS_MS`), then ends. It is paused meanwhile (no timers, game actions rejected with `GAME_PAUSED`, chat works) only if every player who just dropped still has their pause: one per player per game, so nobody can quit the app to look answers up and return with full time. Without a pause the timers keep running. Resuming keeps the remaining time (at least 5 s, `resumeMinMs`) and the pause doesn't count toward answer speed. Clients get `paused` and `waitingForPlayersMs` and show `waiting-for-players`.
- Stopped early (owner, 2026-11-26, built 2026-11-27): when a game stops before the last round (host ends it, too few players, or the 30 s wait runs out) after at least one round has a result, everyone gets the final score screen with the reason (`endedReason`); the unfinished round doesn't count. With no finished round it goes straight to the waiting room. Players who left stay on the final scores with their name and score (`state.departed`, marked "keluar"), also in a game that finishes normally.
- Host loss (verified 2026-11-26): a host offline for 5 s is replaced by the longest-seated online player (any phase) and doesn't get the role back; pressing leave moves it at once; the game goes on with 2+ online. A disconnected member keeps the seat for 30 s; a room whose only member is gone for 30 s is deleted.
- Waiting room: every online player other than the host presses "Siap" before the host can start (the host counts as ready; readiness resets after each game). The host can ping a player who isn't ready (sound `client/public/sounds/ping.mp3`, from Pixabay under the Pixabay Content License: free to use, no attribution; + vibration on Android, once per 10 s per player) and kick players, in the waiting room only (`onMemberLeave` rejects a kick mid-game); a kicked player may join again.
- Up to 10 rounds; the host picks the category (Negara, Hewan, Kota, Buah & Sayur, Profesi, Makanan & Minuman, or a custom "Lainnya"), round count, and answering time (slider: 20, 25, 30, 35 or 40 s; default 30; `answerSeconds`, validated by the server).
- Picking takes 10 s. Letter = `(sum − 1) % 26`; a letter already used skips to the next unused one.
- The reveal lasts up to 10 s (20 s let players look answers up in another tab): each player picks "Ganti huruf" or "Tetap". More than half wanting a change swaps the letter for a random unused one (once per round); half or more keeping it starts answering at once.
- Answering takes the room's answering time (default 30 s):
  - One answer each, in Indonesian (KBBI loanwords count, even when spelled like English: editor, senator; unabsorbed English like "chef" does not), letters and spaces only, no duplicates within a game. Duplicates are compared ignoring spaces, and other names of the same thing count as the same answer (`sameAnswerKey`, `same` maps in `negara.json` and `makanan-minuman.json`; an alias shared by two countries, like "kongo", isn't mapped).
  - Paste is blocked, and answers within 1 s of the phase start are rejected.
  - Answers with swear words are rejected (`INAPPROPRIATE_ANSWER`, counts toward the retry pause) using the base `indonesian-badwords` dictionary + English, so "Anjing"/"Babi" still work; verified answers are never checked. Chat and nicknames use the strict expanded dictionary (`createProfanityChecker({ strict })` in `backend/src/modules/chat/profanity.ts`); nicknames are also checked with digits/`_`/`-` turned into spaces (`INAPPROPRIATE_NAME`). The dictionaries only match whole words, so every checker also strips common suffixes (-nya, -mu, -ku, -lah, -kah, -pun) and flags words that start with a base-dictionary swear word of 5+ letters ("kontolx") or one of a short hand-picked list of English roots (`ENGLISH_PREFIXES`: "fuckx", "shitty"; the full English list as prefixes would hit Indonesian words like analisis); a test keeps every category word list (except Kota, all verified) free of hits.
  - The last player left gets a short final countdown.
- The round result shows for 8 s (`resultMs`, owner 2026-11-28; 5 s hid the penalties below the fold with 8 players).
- Every countdown bar turns red-pen for its last 3 s.
- Voting takes 20 s:
  - Answers found in the category's word list are **verified**: they pass without a vote and can't be rejected; voting is skipped when every answer is verified. Lists live in `backend/src/modules/abc-words/words/` and are built by `backend/scripts/word-lists/*.ts` (Negara from CLDR, Kota from GeoNames CC BY 4.0 minus 13 foreign towns named like everyday words (kuda, singa, cupang…, `COMMON_WORDS` in kota.ts; owner 2026-11-28; the JSON was filtered, not regenerated, because a newer GeoNames dump renames other cities), Hewan from Wordnet Bahasa (MIT) + PWN noun.animal, filtered, plus a hand-written list, Buah & Sayur hand-written, Profesi hand-written and single-word only by the owner's rule ("Dokter" yes, "Dokter Gigi" goes to the vote; juru/kepala/ahli/pemandu/penjaga/pegawai/anggota + X are left out, single-word synonyms like koki, nutrisionis, pramuwisata are in; only pemadam kebakaran, ibu rumah tangga and wali kota are two words; Makanan & Minuman hand-written, single words or reduplications only plus seven approved two-word names (es krim, es teh, es campur, air putih, ayam goreng, pisang goreng, bika ambon), no fruit/vegetables (own category) or alcohol, with a `same` map for spellings of one dish like mi/mie); sources credited on the rules page). Answers not in a list still go to the vote, never auto-fail. In Hewan and Buah & Sayur only base names are verified: "jambu biji" or "ikan emas" go to the vote because "jambu"/"ikan" are listed (`dropQualified`; reduplications like "kura kura" and different animals like "kuda nil" are kept). Negara and Kota keep their multi-word names (Korea Selatan, Padang Panjang are different places).
  - Rejecting needs a reason (off-category / misspelled / not-a-word), and votes are public.
  - Wrong category by the word lists (owner, 2026-11-28, closes the "friends pass each other's junk" hole): an answer not in this category's list but in another one of Negara, Hewan, Buah & Sayur, Profesi or Makanan & Minuman (`otherCategoryOf`; these lists share almost no words) fails at once for −1, without a vote, and can't be rejected (`otherCategory`, shown as "Bukan kategori (Negara)"; error `AUTO_FAILED`). The Kota list is only weak evidence (33k place names include many animals and fruits: singa, kuda, pare…): a place name in another category (`placeName`) is still voted on, but rejecting it is always refunded. "Lainnya" is never checked; every player sees `UNCHECKED_CATEGORY_NOTE` in the waiting room and while voting. Clients use `isJudgeable` (shared/game.ts) for who has something to judge.
  - An answer fails only with **at least 2 rejections and more than half of the other online players**. With 3+ online, one player can never fail an answer alone.
  - Two online (a 2-player game, or only two still connected): the other player is the only judge (`judgedByOne`). One rejection fails an unverified answer, but for 0 points (`POINTS.rejectedByOne`) instead of −1, the rejection is not refunded (so at most 3 answers per game can be blocked), and the lone-rejection rule doesn't apply. Verified answers still pass, and "Lainnya" stays playable.
  - Each player has 3 rejections per game, refunded when the answer fails with support (or when it is a place name, above). Rejecting alone is not penalised (owner, 2026-11-28, option 2a: the honest judge who was outvoted lost points); the quota is the only limit.
- Ranking: score, then the faster average answer time (accepted answers only, ms from the start of answering, measured by the server). Equal on both shares the rank. On the final scores, players with 0 points are ranked by answer time without a trophy (no rank without any accepted answer), and players who left are listed last without a rank, so they can't win.
- Points: accepted +1, failed −1 (also when failed by the word lists; was −2; a failed answer is often just a typo; 0 when only one player judged it), no answer −1. Rejecting alone costs nothing since 2026-11-28 (the ⚠ 2/3 warning and −1 are gone). Scores never go below 0.
- Anti-abuse:
  - Answer spam: 3 rejected tries in 5 s pauses answering for 3 s.
  - Chat spam: mute.
  - AFK: no pick and no answer for 2 rounds, after which the game stops waiting for that player. An AFK player can still reject answers (counted like anyone's) and press "Selesai menilai" (owner, 2026-11-27; server `canJudge`), but voting doesn't wait for them; picking a number or answering makes them active again.
